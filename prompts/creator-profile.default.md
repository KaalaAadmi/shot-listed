# Creator profile (default)

The default text for Settings → **Creator profile**. It describes you and your account for every model call that isn't writing captions. Drafted from your v3.0 Social Media Manager prompt (Sections 2, 5 and 6); edit it in the app and keep it current, especially "What works" and "What doesn't".

Copy everything inside the block below into the setting's default (`web/src/lib/captions/default-voice.ts`, next to the v3.0 prompt).

"Practical limits" reflects what you told me: where you film, that friends sometimes film you, and that final reels are 6–30 seconds. Keep it current; the plan writer and idea coach rely on it for Film it cards.

```text
== CREATOR PROFILE ==

WHO I AM
An Indian professional living in Dublin, posting Instagram Reels from my personal account (about 640 followers when I started, May 2026). I post every 2–3 days. I shoot on an iPhone, sometimes on my own and sometimes with a friend filming, and edit in Instagram's Edits app. No other social media presence.

WHAT MY CONTENT IS
Relatable, funny slice-of-life. The "gym weeb" lifestyle, shown rather than labelled:
- Expat life in Europe as an Indian, played for laughs.
- Anime as gym motivation: characters, references, power-ups.
- Gym life: routines, frustrations, small wins.
- Adulting and desi family expectations, always light.

WHO IT'S FOR
International students and working professionals; Indians abroad and the expat community; anime and gaming fans; gym-goers who like relatable humour. Mostly 25–30.

TONE
Dry, light, quick. One clear joke per reel. Self-aware but not self-pitying. References that insiders get without explanation. Never preachy, never motivational-poster.

WHAT WORKS (from my numbers)
- A simple visual punchline anyone gets in a second (the seagull reel, "Moving Abroad Therapy": my best for views and shares).
- Setup → visual punchline, with the payoff landing fast (Gym Lifestyle / Dips: lower views but very high shares).
- Strong anime community hooks that invite comments (Plan A–M: most comments).
- Clean gym-frustration punchlines (Response to Losing Fat).
- An alter-ego contrast: day self vs night self (Navigating Life / Gaming).

WHAT DOESN'T
- Over-explained concepts with no visual punchline (Dramatic Flashback: my worst).
- Too self-focused, no universal hook (Adulting Joints).
- Telling people my identity instead of showing it.
- Repeating the same format or caption formula back to back.

PRACTICAL LIMITS
- Who films: sometimes a friend films me; sometimes I film myself (tripod or propped phone, front or back camera). Shots that need a second person are fine, but say so.
- Where I film: my home, my gym, the badminton court, around Dublin, and sometimes elsewhere outdoors.
- No paid actors or props beyond what I own; friends can appear occasionally.
- Final reel length: 6–30 seconds.

SUCCESS
Shares first (they bring new people), then comments and saves. Views follow.
```
